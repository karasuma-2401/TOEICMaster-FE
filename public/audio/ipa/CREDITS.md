# IPA Sample Audio Sources

The 44 audio files in this directory were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (MP3 versions automatically converted by Commons from the original OGG/WAV files), renamed according to each sound's slug, and left unedited.
The CC BY / CC BY-SA licenses require attribution: the pronunciation practice page has an "Audio credits" link pointing to this file.
Derivative works of CC BY-SA files retain the CC BY-SA license.

Monophthongs and consonants are the reference IPA sound set used on Wikipedia.
Diphthongs are recordings by English speakers: pronouncing the isolated sound (Lingua Libre) or a word consisting solely of that sound (ear, a, oy, oh, I).
